## Game 2: Block Blast!
**Store link:** https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.block.blast
**Genre:** Block Puzzle
**I played:** 30 minutes, reached score of 3,200 in classic mode

<p>
<img src="Docs/game2/ss1.jpg" width="240">
<img src="Docs/game2/ss2.jpg" width="240">
<img src="Docs/game2/ss3.jpg" width="240">
</p>

1. [M1, M2] Players drag three offered block shapes from bottom tray onto the 8x8 grid.
2. [M3, M4] Clearing full rows or columns awards points and triggers combo animations.
3. [M5, M7] Game ends when none of the three tray blocks fit on the board layout.

| Mechanic | Dynamic | Aesthetic | Bartle type |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1: Drag & Drop Block Placement | Players evaluate grid space to place odd-shaped block pieces | Challenge | Achiever |
| M2: 3-Piece Tray Dealing | Players plan sequence of 3 blocks before committing to grid | Discovery | Achiever |
| M3: Row/Column Line Clearing | Clearing filled lines opens essential grid space for future shapes | Sensation | Achiever |
| M4: Multi-Line Combo Multiplier | Players deliberately fill multiple lines simultaneously for huge score blasts | Challenge | Achiever |
| M5: Grid Spatial Fail-State | Game ends automatically when no space remains for remaining tray pieces | Challenge | Achiever |
| M6: Best Score Persistence | Players restart instantly to surpass personal high score record | Challenge | Achiever |
| M7: Streak Counter | Clearing lines in consecutive turns builds combo sound and visual feedback | Sensation | Achiever |

**Aesthetic profile:** Challenge and Sensation lead through strategic spatial planning without time pressure and high-dopamine blast animations.
**Player types**
Primary: Achiever (Acting x World), because gameplay centers on optimizing grid space and beating high score records.
Secondary: Explorer (Interacting x World), because discovering multi-line combo placements creates satisfying puzzle-solving moments.
